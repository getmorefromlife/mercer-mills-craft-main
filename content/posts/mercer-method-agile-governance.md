How we adapted Scrum and PMP governance frameworks for creative workflows — and why it eliminates the chaos most founders experience with creative teams.

Traditional creative teams rely on "vibes" and email threads. The Mercer Method introduces sprint cycles, daily stand-ups, and a single source of truth for every asset. The result: fewer revision loops, faster delivery, and a clear paper trail for every decision.
