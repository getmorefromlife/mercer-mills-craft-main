**The classic agency trap:** You have a clear vision, a solid budget, and a pressing deadline. Yet, months fly by in a endless loop of kickoff meetings, misaligned scoping documents, and slow progress.

This was exactly the bottleneck the team at **ISTAX** faced. They were trying to get their consulting website off the ground for months, but kept running into bloated agency pipelines that prioritized process over actual shipping.

When they came to Mercer & Mills, we cut through the noise. Instead of endless review cycles, we deployed our senior, seasoned consultants who specialize in high-efficiency sprints. Here is how we did it:

* **Day 1-2: Instant Alignment.** No massive slide decks. We used an async, PMP-led workspace to lock in the site architecture and copywriting directions immediately.
* **Day 3-6: Concurrent Build & Design.** Our developers and designers worked in parallel sprints, building responsive layouts in real-time.
* **Day 7-9: Testing & Launch.** Rigorous cross-browser testing, forms integration, and live deployment.

**The result?** A highly secure, incredibly polished consulting website live in exactly **nine days**.

At Mercer & Mills, we believe that great digital products shouldn’t take half a year to build. By connecting you directly with senior-level consultants who know how to execute, we deliver highly reliable digital products quickly, efficiently, and without the fluff.
