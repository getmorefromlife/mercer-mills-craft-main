A breakdown of our 10-Day Quickstart Sprint — how we take a raw concept to a launch-ready digital product in two weeks flat.

Week one is about discovery and design: user research, wireframes, visual direction. Week two is build and ship: development, QA, and deployment. Every sprint ends with a live product you can show your stakeholders.
